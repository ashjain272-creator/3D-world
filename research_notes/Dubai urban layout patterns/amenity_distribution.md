## 1. Mosques: spacing and catchment standards, counts, placement, plot sizes, landmarks

### Takeaway
Dubai has about 2,450 mosques and prayer spaces for 4.0 to 4.6 million residents, which is roughly 1 per 1,700 to 1,900 people. Around 25 to 30 new mosques open each year. The Dubai Municipality standard cited in the literature is a local (daily-prayer) mosque on a plot of at least 1,800 m² with a service radius of about 500 m. Dubai's code distinguishes small neighbourhood "Awqat" mosques (five daily prayers) from larger "Jumaa" (Friday) mosques. A generator can treat this as a two-tier system: a small mosque every ~500 m walking radius, and a larger Friday mosque per district or community centre.

### Cited Findings
- **Stock:** In November 2024, IACAD's Director-General said Dubai has over 2,450 mosques and prayer spaces serving the public (the count includes prayer spaces, not just purpose-built mosques). — [Media Office / IACAD, Nov 2024](https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2024/november/21-11/iacad-launches-excellence-in-mosque-management-competition-the-first-of-its-kind-regionally)
- **Historical growth:** Mosque numbers grew 36%, from 1,043 in 2008 to 1,418 about four years later. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/going-out/society/36-rise-in-number-of-mosques-in-dubai-1.1061876)
- **Openings per year:**
  - 2024: 24 new mosques, Dh172m, capacity 13,911 worshippers, so about 580 worshippers per mosque on average. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/ramadan/ramadan-gift-dubai-announces-construction-of-55-mosques-expansion-of-friday-sermons-in-english-1.500045780)
  - 2025: 29 new mosques, AED250m. — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/byh09dx-‘dubai-islamic-affairs-department’-opens-new)

- **Pipeline:** 55 mosques under construction, Dh475m, capacity 40,961 (about 745 per mosque). — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/ramadan/ramadan-gift-dubai-announces-construction-of-55-mosques-expansion-of-friday-sermons-in-english-1.500045780). - **Two mosque types in the Dubai Building Code:**

- **Mosques inside master-planned villa communities:**

  - The Arabian Ranches III brochure plan places mosques alongside a central park, retail, clinic and lazy river. — [Emaar](https://www.emaar.com/en/press-release-listing/emaar-breaks-ground-on-arabian-ranches-iii-dubais-happiest-family-destination)
- **Landmark mosques:**
  - Grand Mosque, Bur Dubai (Al Fahidi): capacity about 1,200, minaret about 70 m (tallest in the city historically).   - Jumeirah Mosque (Jumeirah 1, Jumeirah Beach Road): open to visitors via the Sheikh Mohammed Centre for Cultural Understanding. — [Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Al-Jumeirah)
  - Al Farooq Omar Bin Al Khattab Mosque (Al Safa, near Al Wasl Rd) is listed among major Eid-prayer mosques. — [Property Finder](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/celebrate-eid-al-adha-in-5-beautiful-mosques-in-dubai-and-get-to-know-their-neighbourhood/)
  - DIFC Grand Mosque holds about 500; Madinat Badr Masjid (Muhaisnah) about 1,600. — [Bayut](https://bayut.com/mybayut/beautiful-mosques-dubai) (property blog)

### Inferences
- With 2,450 mosques and prayer spaces and 4.0 million (Aug 2025) to 4.58 million (end 2025) residents, there is about 1 mosque per 1,600 to 1,900 residents. (Population source in §8.)
- New-build averages give a size distribution: 2024 openings averaged about 580 worshippers per mosque, and the pipeline averages about 745. Community mosques range from about 200 (villa sub-community) to 780 (dense waterfront district). Landmark and Friday mosques run 1,200 to 1,600+. Suggested generator tiers:
  - neighbourhood: 150–400 worshippers, plot about 1,000–1,800 m²
  - community / Friday: 500–1,500 worshippers, plot about 3,000–5,000 m² (using the Abu Dhabi 4,242 m² Friday-mosque sample as a proxy)
  - landmark: 1,500+
- A spacing rule of roughly 500 m service radius (about 1 mosque per 0.8 km² of residential land under hex packing) is consistent with Dubai Municipality. A stricter 350 m (Abu Dhabi) works for dense old-town areas like Deira and Bur Dubai. Villa communities in practice stretch this to about 600–1,000 m, with the main mosque beside the community centre and retail.
- At about 25–30 openings a year, coverage is continuously backfilled as new communities are built. A generator should give every new residential district at least one mosque plot.

### Gaps
- No primary Dubai Municipality planning-standards table (population per mosque, Friday-mosque radius or plot size) was reachable. The 1,800 m² / 500 m figure is secondhand via MDPI.
- No mosques-per-km² or GIS density figure for Dubai was found.
- No capacity figures were found for Jumeirah Mosque, Al Farooq Mosque or Khalifa Al Tajer Mosque.
- The split between purpose-built mosques and "prayer spaces" (musallas in malls and offices) within the 2,450 figure is unknown.

## 2. Other places of worship: location and whether they are clustered by allocation

### Takeaway
Yes, they are clustered. Non-Muslim worship sites in Dubai sit on land the Ruler donated, grouped in a few walled compounds. The two main clusters are:
- **Oud Metha / Bur Dubai (historic):** St Mary's Catholic Church (land granted about 1965–67) and Holy Trinity Anglican (1970), next to each other on Oud Metha Road. The historic Hindu temple (1958) is in the Bur Dubai souk.
- **Jebel Ali "Worship Village" / Corridor of Tolerance (newer):** about 7 churches, Guru Nanak Darbar Sikh Gurudwara and the Hindu temple (opened 2022).

Federal Law No. 9 of 2023 requires licensing of all non-Muslim places of worship.

### Cited Findings
- **Hindu temple, Bur Dubai:** In 1958 Sheikh Rashid bin Saeed permitted a Hindu temple to be built on the roof of the Bur Dubai souk. — [Hinduism Today](https://www.hinduismtoday.com/hpi/?p=14362)
- **St Mary's Catholic Church:** Land was donated by Sheikh Rashid about 1965/66; the church was established 1967 and rebuilt 1989. It is on Oud Metha Road opposite the Indian High School. Sources disagree on the exact grant year. — [Bayut](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/st-marys-church-dubai/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/church-in-dubai-celebrates-its-golden-jubilee-1.69644808)
- **Holy Trinity Church (Anglican):** Sheikh Rashid allocated land in Oud Metha. The foundation stone was laid 12 March 1970 (Wikipedia gives founding 5 April 1970). It is in a high-walled compound beside St Mary's. — [Dubai As It Used To Be](https://dubaiasitusedtobe.net/ChuckPringleHolyTrinityChurch.shtml); [Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Holy_Trinity_Church,_Dubai)
- **Jebel Ali worship village:**
  - It houses nine shrines: seven churches, the Guru Nanak Darbar Sikh Gurudwara and the new Hindu temple. — [Hinduism Today](https://www.hinduismtoday.com/hpi/?p=18970)
  - The Jebel Ali church complex is on land donated by Sheikh Hamdan bin Rashid. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/dubai-churches-mourn-sheikh-hamdans-death-recall-his-donation-of-land-for-jebel-ali-church-complex-1.78065918)
  - The Sikh gurdwara is on land donated by the government / Sheikh Mohammed. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/going-out/society/shining-example-of-tolerance-1.1507390)
  - The Hindu temple in Jebel Ali opened October 2022 on land donated by Sheikh Mohammed; one source says about 4 acres (about 1.6 ha). — [The National](https://thenationalnews.com/uae/heritage/2022/10/04/symbol-of-unity-hindu-temple-that-blends-indian-and-arabic-design-opens-in-dubai); [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/hindu-temple-jebel-ali/) (travel blog)
  - The area is described as the "Corridor of Tolerance". — [Bayut](https://bayut.com/mybayut/corridor-tolerance-jebel-ali); [Wikipedia: Churches Complex](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Churches_Complex)
- **Land model:** Christian churches and Hindu and Sikh temples serving the noncitizen population operate on land donated by the ruling families. — [US State Dept 2020 IRF Report via ecoi.net](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2051727.html)
- **Law:**
  - Federal Law No. 9 of 2023 regulates non-Muslim houses of worship nationwide, including free zones, with licensing by a local competent authority. "Rooms of worship" may be allocated in coordination with that authority. — [UAE Legislation](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2588/download); [The National](https://thenationalnews.com/uae/government/2023/05/31/uae-sets-out-plan-to-regulate-non-muslim-places-of-worship)
  - Fines run up to Dh750,000 for violations. — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae-penalties-non-muslim-places-worship-regulations)
- **National count:** The UAE has 73 licensed non-Muslim places of worship. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/uae-hosts-73-non-muslim-places-of-worship-1.500276866)

### Inferences
- Generator rule: do not scatter churches and temples. Place them in one or two walled multi-faith compounds, each holding several (about 5–9) buildings on adjacent plots:
  - one in the old core next to the souk district (Oud Metha / Bur Dubai analogue)
  - one at the far industrial and port edge of the city (Jebel Ali analogue, about 35–40 km from the old core)
- Ratio: about 2,450 mosques against a few dozen non-Muslim sites, so roughly 100+ mosques per church or temple.

### Gaps
- No Dubai-only count of churches and temples was found; the 73 is UAE-wide.
- No plot areas were found for the Oud Metha compound.
- The Bur Dubai Shiva/Krishna temple was relocated to Jebel Ali in 2024, but I found no source confirming this, so it is not stated as fact.

## 3. Malls: hierarchy, size, spacing relative to highways and metro

### Takeaway
Dubai's retail is dominated by a few super-regional malls (over 74,000 m² GLA), each anchoring a node on Sheikh Zayed Road or another major artery and typically with a direct metro link. Supply is about 54% super-regional, 33% regional and 13% community (2017). Mall density is about 1,214 m² GLA per 1,000 residents, among the highest in the world. Villa communities have small community centres and "souks" with about 20–80 units.

### Cited Findings
- **ICSC tiers used for Dubai:**
  - super-regional: over 74,000 m² GLA, 3+ anchors
  - regional: 37,000–74,000 m², 2+ anchors
  - community: 9,300–32,500 m²
  - neighbourhood: 2,800–13,900 m²
  - — [Oxford Business Group](https://oxfordbusinessgroup.com/reports/uae-dubai/2019-report/economy/shopping-centre-dubai-is-set-to-hold-onto-its-position-as-a-retail-leader-despite-continuing-challenges)
- **Supply mix (Q1 2017):** about 54% super-regional, 33% regional, 13% community. — [Knight Frank Dubai Retail Overview](https://content.knightfrank.com/research/1267/documents/en/dubai-retail-overview-4708.pdf)
- **Density:** 1,214 m² GLA per 1,000 people, second only to New York (Core Savills). Dubai's per-capita mall space is about 4.5× the UK. — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/retail/dubai-abu-dhabi-continue-to-be-mall-magnets); [OBG](https://oxfordbusinessgroup.com/overview/shopping-centre-dubai-set-hold-its-position-retail-leader-despite-continuing-challenges)
- **The Dubai Mall (Downtown, off SZR):** about 3.77 million sq ft (about 350,000 m²) GLA, 1,200+ stores. — [Burj Khalifa / Emaar](https://burjkhalifa.ae/en/downtown-dubai/dubai-mall); [Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Dubai_Mall)

- **Mall of the Emirates (Al Barsha, on SZR):** GLA 234,470 m², total area 600,000 m². — [The Business Year](https://thebusinessyear.com/?p=21633)
  - Red Line station opened 9 Sep 2009, linked by an enclosed overhead walkway. — [Wikipedia: MoE station](https://Www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mall_of_the_Emirates_(Dubai_Metro))
- **Ibn Battuta Mall (Jebel Ali / Discovery Gardens, on SZR):** 210 m two-storey walkway to its metro station, opened November 2016. — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2016/industry/new-link-opens-dubais-ibn-battuta-mall-metro-station/); [Ibn Battuta Mall](https://www.ibnbattutamall.com/en/news/nakheel-enhances-the-ibn-battuta-journey-with-opening-of-mall-metro-connection)

- **Community retail inside villa communities:**
  - Arabian Ranches community centre: about 20 outlets plus mosque and clinic. — [Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Arabian_Ranches)
  - Ranches Souk (Arabian Ranches 2): 35+ units on two floors (another source says 80+ outlets; sources conflict). — [metropolitan.realestate](https://metropolitan.realestate/areas/dubai/arabian-ranches/)

### Inferences
- **GLA budget:** Using 1,214 m² per 1,000 people, about 4.9 million m² of mall GLA for 4 million people.
- **Generator hierarchy:**
  - 3–5 super-regional malls (250,000–350,000 m² GLA), each on the main highway spine (SZR analogue), spaced roughly 10–20 km apart:
    - Dubai Mall: Downtown, about km 12 from the old core
    - Mall of the Emirates: about 10 km further SW
    - Ibn Battuta: about 13 km further SW
    - (spacing from general geography; no source found)
  - Each super-regional mall connects to a metro station by an enclosed bridge of about 200–800 m.
  - Several regional and community malls (10,000–70,000 m²) per sub-city.
  - A neighbourhood centre (2,800–14,000 m²) or community souk in every villa community, co-located with its main mosque and clinic.

### Gaps

- No official spacing rule for retail centres was found.

## 4. Souks

### Takeaway
Traditional souks cluster on both banks of Dubai Creek at its mouth, connected by abra (water taxi):
- **Deira side (north/east):** Gold Souk, Spice Souk and Perfume Souk along Baniyas St / Al Ras near the Old Souk abra station.
- **Bur Dubai side (south/west):** Textile Souk, beside the Grand Mosque and Al Fahidi.

Modern "souk-style" malls (Souk Madinat Jumeirah, Ranches Souk) are resort or community pastiches located elsewhere.

### Cited Findings
- **Deira Gold Souk:** along Baniyas Street near the Al Ras / Old Souk abra station. — [Bayut](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/best-souks-in-dubai)
- **Spice Souk:** a few minutes' walk south of the Gold Souk. — [Visit Dubai](https://visitdubai.com/da/articles/ultimate-guide-to-dubai-souks); [Qantas](https://qantas.com/travelinsider/en/explore/middle-east/united-arab-emirates/dubai/deiras-souks.html)
- **Textile Souk (Bur Dubai):** directly across the Creek from the Gold, Spice and Perfume souks; reached by abra. — [Expedia](https://www.expedia.co.uk/stories/the-best-souks-in-dubai)
- **Grand Mosque position:** between the Textile Souk and the Dubai Museum. — [Bayut](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/grand-mosque-dubai-guide)
- **Souk Madinat Jumeirah:** King Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud St, Al Sufouh, part of the Madinat Jumeirah resort, a modern recreation. — [Hidubai](https://focus.hidubai.com/ultimate-guide-to-dubai-souks)

### Inferences
- **Generator rule:** souks are dense, narrow-lane, covered-arcade clusters only in the historic creek-side core (each roughly a few hundred metres across). They face each other across the creek, with abra stations and the historic grand mosque adjacent.
- Souk-styled retail elsewhere appears only as resort or community-centre pastiche.

### Gaps
- No footprint areas for the souk districts were found.

## 5. Parks: hierarchy, sizes, Dubai 2040 targets

### Takeaway
Dubai Municipality runs about 211–220 public parks and facilities totalling about 13 km². Five major "city" parks dominate:
- Mushrif: about 500+ ha, desert-forest, eastern edge
- Creek: 96 ha, on the Creek
- Zabeel: about 51 ha, between Karama and SZR
- Safa: 1975, Al Wasl / SZR
- Al Mamzar: beach park near the Sharjah border

The rest are community and neighbourhood parks plus many small playgrounds. Dubai 2040 targets:
- green and recreational space roughly doubled (+105%)
- 310 new parks by 2040
- public beaches extended 400% (21 km to 105 km)
- 60% of the emirate as nature reserves or rural natural areas

### Cited Findings
- **Park count and area:**
  - Over 220 public parks and recreational facilities. — [DM](https://www.dm.gov.ae/?p=361818)
  - 211 public parks (Aug 2025). — [Media Office](https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2025/august/03-08/dubai-municipality-parks-and-facilities-attract-over-16-million-visitors)
  - Combined area of all public parks and facilities is about 13 million m² (2023). — [DM](https://www.dm.gov.ae/?p=291563)
- **Park sizes:**
  - Mushrif Park: over 500 ha, about 5 km² of sandy forest. — [Living in Dubai](https://livingindubai.org/everything-about-dubai-municipal-parks/); [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/lifestyle/family/from-the-palm-to-the-creek-dubai-s-10-best-parks-1.804728)
  - Creek Park: 96 ha. — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/lifestyle/family/from-the-palm-to-the-creek-dubai-s-10-best-parks-1.804728)
  - Zabeel Park: 51 ha. — [Planetware](https://planetware.com/united-arab-emirates/best-parks-in-dubai-uae-1-30.htm) (travel site)
  - Safa Park: dates from 1975, once the city's largest green space; no area found. — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/lifestyle/family/from-the-palm-to-the-creek-dubai-s-10-best-parks-1.804728)
- **Building pace:**
  - 70 new parks and playgrounds were completed in one year (2020). — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/dubai-municipality-has-built-70-new-parks-and-playgrounds-in-the-emirate-this-year/)
  - 35 parks are due to open in 2026; the target is 310 new parks by 2040 plus upgrades of 220 existing ones. — [The National, Apr 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/04/05/dubai-to-open-35-parks-this-year/)
- **Dubai 2040 targets:**
  - Green and recreational spaces double (+105%), public beach length +400%, nature reserves and rural areas 60% of the emirate. — [u.ae](https://u.ae/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/transport-and-infrastructure/dubai-2040-urban-master-plan); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/news/video-60-of-dubai-to-be-nature-reserves-says-sheikh-mohammed)
  - Five main urban centres. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/photos/news/dubai-2040-urban-master-plan-five-urban-centres-in-dubai-to-be-focus-of-investment-1.1615639613110?slide=4)
- **20-minute city (Dubai 2040 Phase II, Dec 2022):** 80% of daily needs reachable within 20 minutes on foot or by bike; 55% of residents within 800 m of a mass-transit station. A model district is under construction in Al Barsha 2. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/mohammed-bin-rashid-approves-phase-ii-of-dubai-2040-urban-master-plan-with-20-minute-city-policy-1.92651013); [The National 2025](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2025/06/26/how-dubais-20-minute-city-ambitions-are-becoming-reality)
- **Villa communities:** Arabian Ranches III centres on a central park with sports courts, splash pads and a lazy river. — [Emaar](https://www.emaar.com/en/press-release-listing/emaar-breaks-ground-on-arabian-ranches-iii-dubais-happiest-family-destination)

### Inferences
- **Average park size:** 13 km² / about 215 parks ≈ 6 ha mean. The distribution is extremely skewed: Mushrif alone is about 5 km², roughly 40% of the total.
- **Generator park hierarchy:**
  - 1 desert / forest mega-park, about 500 ha, at the urban edge
  - 3–4 city parks of 50–100 ha in the older core (Creek, Zabeel, Safa analogues), adjacent to the creek or highway
  - community parks of about 2–10 ha, one per community, often central
  - neighbourhood and pocket parks / playgrounds of 0.1–1 ha, frequent (dozens per year built)
- **Implied current per-capita figure:** about 13 km² for 4 million people ≈ 3.2 m² per person. Doubling under the 2040 plan would bring it to about 6.5 m² per person at today's population.

### Gaps
- No official Dubai park-provision standard (m² per person, park catchment radius) was found; it may be in the MDPI paper or DM standards (unreachable).
- No Safa Park or Al Mamzar areas were found.

## 6. Sports venues

### Takeaway
Major venues are spread along the highway corridors rather than in one cluster:
- Coca-Cola Arena: 17,000, City Walk, near Downtown/Jumeirah between SZR and Al Wasl
- Hamdan Sports Complex: 15,000, Nad Al Sheba / Al Hebiah, inland
- Dubai International Cricket Stadium: 25,000, in Dubai Sports City, a purpose-built sports district inland off E311 / Al Khail
- Meydan racecourse: Nad Al Sheba
- Football club stadiums of about 8–12k: Al Wasl's Zabeel Stadium and others, embedded in older districts

### Cited Findings

## 7. Transport: metro, tram, stations, highways

### Takeaway
- **Metro:** 2 lines, about 89.6 km, 55 stations (Gulf Today's count of 64 likely includes tram stops).
  - Red Line: about 67 km, 35 stations, runs parallel to SZR from Rashidiya/airport to Jebel Ali / Expo City.
  - Green Line: 22.5 km, 20 stations, loops through the old town (Deira / Bur Dubai) on both sides of the Creek.
  - Blue Line (30 km) under construction will bring rail to 131 km and 78 stations.
- **Tram:** about 10.6 km, 11 stops, Al Sufouh–Dubai Marina–JBR.
- **Highways:**
  - Sheikh Zayed Road (E11): 7–8 lanes each way, the coastal spine
  - parallel inland arterials: Al Khail Rd (E44), Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Rd (E311), Emirates Rd (E611)

  - Green Line: 22.5 / 20 ≈ 1.1 km. It is denser in the old town and partly underground under the Creek.

- **Generator:**
  - a main coastal highway spine (16 lanes total) with an elevated metro in or beside the median, stations every 1.5–2 km, each with a pedestrian bridge

  - 2–3 parallel inland highways about 5–15 km apart (E44 / E311 / E611 analogues), connected by perpendicular arterials at major interchanges
  - a short tram loop in the marina / beach high-rise district
  - super-regional malls at metro stations on the spine (see §3)

## 8. Hotels and beachfront

### Takeaway
Dubai has about 152,000–159,000 hotel rooms in about 770–820 establishments (2025), about 70% high-end, with about 81% occupancy. Public beach is only about 21 km today. The 2023 Public Beaches Master Plan extends it to 105 km by 2040; Phase I covers 54 km, and Jebel Ali Beach will be 6.6 km / 330 ha. Hotels concentrate on the coast (Jumeirah, Palm, Marina/JBR) and in Downtown and Deira.

### Cited Findings
- **Hotel inventory:**
  - 158,700 rooms in 770 establishments in 2025, about 70% high-end, 81% average occupancy. — [Cavendish Maxwell](https://cavendishmaxwell.com/news/dubai-hotel-market-expands-to-158700-rooms-as-luxury-segment-dominates)
  - Alternative count: about 152,300 rooms in 818 hotels (Jan–Aug 2025). — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/gcc/dubais-hotel-inventory-grows-to-nearly-152-300-rooms-in-2025-lojyyvl1)

- About 35–40 hotel rooms per 1,000 residents. The average establishment has about 200 rooms.
